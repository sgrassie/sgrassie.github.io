**Effective date: 27 October 2026**

Matchistry is an informational YouTube channel. This policy explains how the
channel operator handles information shared through the channel or by email.

## YouTube

Google and YouTube process information about your use of YouTube under their
own privacy practices. This can include your activity and device information.
Read the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) to learn
more. Use [Google privacy controls](https://myaccount.google.com/privacycheckup)
to review the controls for your account.

Comments and other interactions on YouTube can be public, subject to the
platform's settings. The person who posts a comment can edit or delete it.
Channel operators can moderate comments and remove them. See
[YouTube's comment help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6000976?hl=en)
for details.

## Email

If you email the channel operator, the message has the information you choose
to provide, such as your email address. The operator uses it to reply to you
and manage your enquiry. The operator keeps it only as long as needed for these
purposes.

For privacy questions or requests about information you sent by email, contact
[developer@fy7softwarelimited.co.uk](mailto:developer@fy7softwarelimited.co.uk).
Requests about information held in your Google or YouTube account must be made
through Google or YouTube. The [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
explains how to review their privacy controls and make requests.
